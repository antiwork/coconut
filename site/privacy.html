Coconut

Privacy

Updated November 17, 2026

Coconut is an iPhone and Mac app for your Hermes agents. Your agents, chats, files and settings live on your own Mac. We do not run a server that stores them, and we do not collect data about you.

What stays on your devices

Your messages, attachments, agent memory, Library pages and settings are stored on your Mac and on your paired iPhone. Your agents call the AI providers that you configure in Hermes, under your own accounts and their terms.

The relay

Your iPhone reaches your Mac through a relay that Coconut runs on Cloudflare. The connection is end-to-end encrypted between your iPhone and your Mac, so the relay cannot read what passes through it. The relay also delivers notifications through Apple Push Notification service. The Mac encrypts each notification preview, and only your iPhone can decrypt it. The relay keeps no message content and does not log request bodies.

Teams

When you join a team, the Macs of its members exchange the messages and pages that you share with it, encrypted with the team's keys. The members of the team can read what you share with them.

Analytics and ads

Coconut has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The demo

The demo shows sample data on your iPhone. It sends nothing anywhere.
